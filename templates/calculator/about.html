How it works

Every scene has a brightness, measured as an exposure value at ISO 100 (EV100). A sunny day is about EV 15, a normal room about EV 7.

Aperture, shutter speed and ISO are linked to it by:

EV100 = log2(N² / t) − log2(ISO / 100)

Pick the light and two settings, and the third is calculated, then rounded to the nearest value a real camera offers (1/3-stop steps).

API

GET /api/exposure/?light=12&aperture=5.6&iso=200
